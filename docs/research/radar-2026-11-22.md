# Radar 2026-11-22: upstream and community changes since the 11-14 sweep

> **Summary.** One find matters most. The Iris Xe hung 20 times on 11-20 and 11-21, 19 of them on the CCS
> (compute) ring, and `mediaanalysisd` was the app in 12 of them. That is WhateverXe #163, Apple enabling the CCS
> against Intel's Gen12 LP workaround. Community forks fix it with `-wex-noccs`. Other reports say compositing stays
> slow after a recovered hang until reboot, which would explain lag that comes back. Next: port `-wex-noccs` (XE-14),
> then rebase on WhateverXe 0.8.3 and try the 144 Hz fix another A7A8 laptop just published.

## What runs here (checked 2026-11-22)

- macOS 15.8.1; WhateverXe 0.8.2 (ours, `-wex-objpreempt` LAT-8), Lilu 1.7.2, nullmoth NVRM/NVAccel 3.0,
  AirportMtkWlan 1.5.0 [measured, `kextstat`, boot-args].
- Claude desktop 6.6395.1 on Electron 45.3.3 / Chrome 156 [measured, Info.plist, framework strings].

## Finding 1: CCS hangs from `mediaanalysisd` [measured here]

- `/Library/Logs/DiagnosticReports`: 20 `.gpuRestart` reports, 16 on 11-20 (16:46-16:49, one every ~10 s) and 4 on
  11-21 (16:16-16:54). Hung ring: CCS in 19, RCS in 1. `Application:` is `mediaanalysisd` in 12, `WindowServer` in 1, and
  empty in 7. A `WindowServer ... userspace_watchdog_timeout.spin` was written at 11-21 16:17, during the burst.
- Our `/Library/Extensions/AppleIntelTGLGraphics.kext` Info.plist has `EnableMultiContext => 1` twice. The binary
  holds the key string once [measured, `plutil`, `strings`].
- [WhateverXe #163](https://github.com/b00t0x/WhateverXe/issues/163) (RPL-HX, 11-18): `EnableMultiContext = 1` puts
  Metal compute on the CCS. Intel's TGL PRM Vol 14 workaround "SVSM: Dual Context - Invalidate hang" says the
  scheduler must not run CCS and RCS in parallel, and Linux i915 exposes no CCS on Gen12 LP. Making the key read as its
  default 0 makes the driver log `Multi-Context (Compute Command Streamer) Disabled!` and stops the hangs. Vision,
  Live Text and `mediaanalysisd`/`photoanalysisd` are the triggers.
- Ports: `-wex-noccs` in [skmaiti2050/WhateverXe-TigerLakeH](https://github.com/skmaiti2050/WhateverXe-TigerLakeH/commits/wex-0.8.2-noccs)
  (`f2d016b` + `bd5940e`: Lilu's search window must be one byte longer than the key), `-wex-g15-noccs` in
  [rodgod-rr/WhateverXe](https://github.com/rodgod-rr/WhateverXe/tree/g15-deep-idle) (`315305d`). Upstream has no
  flag yet [measured, README at 0.8.3].
- Why it may be our lag [inferred, not yet measured here]: [#171](https://github.com/b00t0x/WhateverXe/issues/171)
  (ADL-H) and [#179](https://github.com/b00t0x/WhateverXe/issues/179) (TGL-H) report that after recovered hangs,
  window dragging and compositing stay slow until a reboot. Logging out does not clear it. #171's hang signature
  (`IPEHR=0x61040001`, `ACTHD=0x0a000140`) is the one #163 traces to the CCS. #179 also saw Reduce Transparency +
  Reduce Motion help "noticeably". Our LAT runs never checked for hangs earlier in the same boot. Pitfall to add if
  XE-14 confirms it.

## Finding 2: WhateverXe 0.8.3 (2026-11-17) and the jkbuha contrib branch

- [0.8.3](https://github.com/b00t0x/WhateverXe/releases/tag/0.8.3): fixes a GPU recovery failure that could stall a
  restart after a hang (`a760a78`, blit recovery). It also improves ADL/RPL eDP at startup and after display sleep and
  adds experimental VESA AUX brightness on ADL/RPL eDP. Graphics 0.4.4 only fixes H.264 encode (we run 0.4.3's
  payload [unverified which payload is installed]).
- [jkbuha/WhateverXe-contrib](https://github.com/jkbuha/WhateverXe-contrib) `contrib/xps-mobile-xelp` (0.8.3
  merged): `a738432` balances the hang report's GT forcewake so that RC6 works again after a recovered hang, `926f7c8`
  recovers Gen12 host-execlist hangs instead of freezing, and `f3ae2c4` reports work killed by a reset as failed. All
  of them are about hang recovery, so they matter only while hangs happen.
- `rodgod-rr` `cc10780` (11-22): i915's RCS context workarounds for GRAPHICS 12.00 in the per-context batch. It is a
  candidate if RCS hangs remain after XE-14 [unverified for RPL-P].

## Finding 3: 144 Hz on the same iGPU (A7A8)

- [#182](https://github.com/b00t0x/WhateverXe/issues/182) (MSI Thin 15, i7-13620H, `8086:A7A8` rev 04, 1080p 144 Hz
  eDP, no MUX): native framebuffer plus Metal at 144 Hz after two fixes. The VBT duplicate on DDI A no longer counts
  as `Conflict`, and the watermark check allows 2 pixels per CDCLK (i915's display 10+ rule). It is merged in jkbuha
  `a63520b` with tests. EFI and patch: [small-jp/MSI-Thin15-B13VE-Hackintosh](https://github.com/small-jp/MSI-Thin15-B13VE-Hackintosh).
- Fits open problem 3 (desktop at 60 Hz). Check first whether our failure is the same one (`N97FBWMGeometryFailure`
  pixelHz vs nativeHz, `N97NativeRoutingUnavailable`). It is on Tahoe there [unverified on Sequoia].

## Finding 4: GT frequency without GuC

- [#176](https://github.com/b00t0x/WhateverXe/issues/176) (Acer Nitro Lite 16, A7A8, RTX 3050 on nullmoth 3.0, the
  same mix as ours): with `-disablegfxfirmware` the host-RPS answer is always RP0 (1300-1500 MHz even at idle). A
  governor driven by engine activity saves ~1 W at idle with no UI slowdown. A comment proposes the RC6 counter
  `0x138108` instead of polling. This is power and heat, not latency. Our earlier "~1100 MHz granted vs 1400
  requested" may be the package limit at work [inferred].
